## 1. AIA Engineering's competitive landscape and customer base

AIA holds 90-95% market share in India and 35-40% globally (excluding China). Source: [Sharescart Equity Research, AIA Engineering](https://www.sharescart.com/article/equity-research-aia-engineering)

Source: [Vega / AIA company profile, Kompass](https://us.kompass.com/c/aia-engineering-limited/in771761/); [AIA Engineering official site](https://aiaengineering.com/investor-contact-us/)

## 3. AI in the comminution / grinding circuit specifically

### Why this section matters most for AIA

- Comminution (crushing + grinding) is the single biggest energy user at a mine site, accounting for up to ~50% of mine site energy consumption and ~3% of global primary energy according to CEEC analysis. Source: [CEEC: Mining Energy Consumption 2021](https://www.ceecthefuture.org/resources/mining-energy-consumption-2021)
- Reducing comminution energy is the largest single ESG and cost lever in mining; AI optimisation, HPGR, and stirred mills can cut energy consumption by up to 40%. Source: [Mine magazine, June 2025: Reducing emissions and energy use in comminution](https://mine.nridigital.com/mine_jun25/reducing-emissions-energy-comminution); [Weir 2025: Crushing and grinding with Bjorn Dierx](https://www.global.weir/newsroom/global-news/2025/crushing-and-grinding-with-bjorn-dierx/)

### Documented AI throughput / energy gains in grinding circuits

- McKinsey-cited industrial-AI deployments in processing plants report 10-15% production increases and 4-5% EBITA improvement in documented case studies, with throughput uplift of 4-8% specifically in grinding circuits. Source: [Imubit: AI in mining operations](https://imubit.com/article/ai-mining-operations/)
- A digital-twin-based AI optimiser deployed alongside an APC layer at a large gold mine delivered a 1% throughput increase worth USD 3-4 million per year in topline impact, after the original APC investment had stalled. Source: [SymphonyAI mining and metals case study](https://www.symphonyai.com/resources/case-study/industrial/mining-and-metals/)
- Chilean copper SAG-mill optimisation papers using ML and Bayesian networks consistently demonstrate measurable production and power gains. Sources: [MDPI: SAG grinding optimisation, statistical and ML, Chilean copper mining](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1944/16/8/3220); [MDPI: Bayesian networks SAG mill production and power](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-163X/16/1/60)

### Vendor landscape (the people selling AI into AIA's customers' mills)

- **Metso Geminex** (digital twin combining physical plant simulation + AI for online monitoring, operator training, and long/mid-term ore-body and process optimisation). December 2025 launch of a modular Grinding Classification System sitting alongside Geminex. Sources: [Metso Geminex page](https://www.metso.com/mining/solutions/geminex/); [Metso Dec 2025 modular grinding launch](https://www.metso.com/corporate/media/news/2025/12/metso-launches-a-modular-grinding-classification-system-for-maximized-implementation-efficiency/); [International Mining: Metso Geminex 2025](https://im-mining.com/2025/12/13/metso-and-the-process-plant-optimisation-power-of-geminex/)
- **ABB Ability Expert Optimizer** (model predictive advanced process control on System 800xA DCS for grinding and flotation), plus Predictive Maintenance for Grinding service. Source: [ABB Ability Expert Optimizer for grinding/flotation](https://new.abb.com/mining/mineoptimize/digital-applications/advanced-process-control/abb-ability-expert-optimizer); [ABB Predictive Maintenance for grinding](https://new.abb.com/mining/services/advanced-digital-services/predictive-maintenance-grinding)
- **Honeywell Forge** smart APC for water and process optimisation in mining. Source: [Honeywell Forge mining case study PDF](https://process.honeywell.com/content/dam/process/en/industries/mining-minerals-metals/documents/hon-ia-hce-water-mgmt-case-study.pdf)
- **Weir Minerals NEXT Intelligent Solutions and Synertrex IIoT platform**, with a 2025 partnership with Sweden's Viking Analytics for AI vibration analysis and predictive maintenance. Sources: [Weir NEXT digital twin](https://www.global.weir/services/digital-services/NEXT-intelligent-solutions/); [Weir + Viking Analytics 2025 partnership](https://www.global.weir/newsroom/global-news/2025/weir-partners-with-viking-analytics-to-enhance-digital-offering-for-mineral-processing-plants/)
- **Petuum Industrial AI / Imubit / SymphonyAI Industrial** all selling vertical AI optimisation directly into mineral processing plants. Source: [Imubit AI for SAG mills](https://imubit.com/article/sag-mill/); [SymphonyAI](https://www.symphonyai.com/resources/case-study/industrial/mining-and-metals/)

### Has AIA itself published anything on smart liners or instrumented media?

- No public material found from AIA Engineering on instrumented grinding media, IoT-enabled mill liners, or digital twins of their own products. Their public technical narrative is metallurgy (alloys, hardness, wear performance), not data.
- Meanwhile, academic and commercial work is racing ahead: instrumented grinding media with embedded triaxial accelerometers can characterise mill load and operation in real time. Source: [ScienceDirect: Assessing load in ball mill using instrumented grinding media](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0892687521004271)
- IoT shell-liner wear sensors coupled with Discrete Element Modelling can predict and continuously update liner wear patterns. Source: [ScienceDirect: A novel IoT sensor and evolution model for grinding mill liner wear monitoring](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0892687524003881); [SSRN preprint](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4833315)
- ABB has published ML methods solving the same liner-monitoring problem. Source: [ABB news: Machine learning solves grinding mill liner monitoring](https://new.abb.com/news/detail/32597/machine-learning-solves-grinding-mill-liner-monitoring)

**What this means for the AIA pitch:** This is the killer slide. Every layer around AIA's product (the mill OEM, the process control system, the IoT sensor layer, the digital twin, even the bolt holding the liner in place) is being instrumented and AI-optimised by someone else. AIA's product itself remains a "dumb" consumable. The strategic question to put to Bhadresh Shah: do you want your liners and media to be the data source, or do you want the customer's AI vendor (Metso, ABB, Microsoft) to own the relationship and treat AIA as a swappable input?

## 5. AI in foundry / metallurgy operations (AIA's own factory)

### Foundry-specific AI applications now production-ready

- Deep-learning detection of small-scale casting defects via X-ray imaging, including dual-layer encoder-decoder multi-scale architectures and Wasserstein-distance loss functions. Source: [Nature Scientific Reports: detection of small casting defects](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-90185-y)
- Real-time defect prediction trained on casting process data and mould geometries, running 100x faster than legacy methods, deployed directly into production for higher yields and faster throughput. Source: [PhysicsX: Real-time intelligence in the foundry](https://www.physicsx.ai/newsroom/real-time-intelligence-in-the-foundry-ai-for-casting-quality-and-efficiency)
- AI-enhanced inspection in pump impellers and similar high-value castings via transfer learning. Source: [SAGE Journal of Engineering Computations: AI-enhanced casting defect detection](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/1063293X251363430)
- Anomaly-detection plus semantic segmentation hybrid approach for casting quality. Source: [MDPI Machines: intelligent casting quality inspection](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-1702/13/4/317)
- AI visual inspection market grew from USD 15.48 bn in 2023 to projected USD 89.73 bn by 2033, 19.62% CAGR, dominated by manufacturing quality control. Source: [Tricon Infotech: AI for quality control in manufacturing](https://www.triconinfotech.com/insights/ai-quality-control-manufacturing-defect-detection/)

### Indian peer benchmark: Tata Steel

- Tata Steel has built ~600 AI tools and 260+ AI algorithms over its digital journey since 2018, with 11.2 PB of data ingested, AI-enabled digital twins, predictive maintenance, and process optimisation. Source: [Business Standard: Tata Steel built 550+ AI models](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/built-over-550-ai-models-in-5-6-yrs-to-enhance-output-quality-tata-steel-125020401386_1.html); [AIX case study: Tata Steel AI Transformation](https://aiexpert.network/case-study-tata-steels-ai-transformation/)
- Tata Steel set a 2018 roadmap to lead digital steelmaking by 2025 and to extract USD 2 bn in savings via digital, which it reports having achieved. Source: [CIO.inc: Tata Steel Mission 2025](https://www.cio.inc/tata-steel-mission-2025-lead-digital-steelmaking-a-19996)
- Harvard Business School case study published on the transformation. Source: [HBS case: Digital Transformation at Tata Steel](https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/item.aspx?num=63314)

### Indian peer benchmark: JSW Steel

- JSW Steel's Vijayanagar plant runs thousands of sensors monitoring heat, pressure, vibration; ML algorithms detect mechanical stress and recommend preventive interventions. Now described as integral to daily operations per JSW's 2023-24 annual report. Source: [Markintel: How AI is redefining the steel industry](https://markintel.co.in/how-ai-is-redefining-the-steel-industry-from-blast-furnaces-to-business-intelligence/); [Steel-Technology.com: top steel companies adopting AI](https://www.steel-technology.com/articles/top-6-steel-companies-adopting-ai)

Already deploying predictive maintenance, intelligent thermal monitoring, automated process optimisation, advanced froth analytics, and AI-powered energy management. Source: [Machine Maker: Vedanta targets USD 400m AI value](https://themachinemaker.com/news/vedanta-targets-usd-400-million-value-creation-through-ai-led-industrial-transformation-initiatives/)

Tata Steel went from "no AI" to USD 2 bn in savings in seven years. The pitch frame: "your direct Indian metals peers are not piloting AI; they are mid-stream on second-generation AI deployments. The board-level question is whether AIA wants to lead this curve or follow it after a competitor uses it to take pricing power."

## Bottom line for the deck (one paragraph you can actually use)

AIA Engineering operates inside a tightening sandwich. Above it, every named global mining major (BHP, Rio, Vale, Anglo, Codelco, Newmont, Barrick) is publicly committed at board level to AI-led mill optimisation, autonomous operations, and integrated decision systems, with named vendor partners running the layer that controls grinding-circuit parameters. Beside it, both of AIA's main global competitors (Magotteaux with MagoSense, Molycop with Digital Engine and MillSlicer VIP) and the #4 (ME Elecmetal with SecureFIT) have launched branded digital products positioning themselves beyond consumables. Below it, OEMs like Metso, ABB and Weir are bundling hardware with AI software, threatening to relegate the consumable supplier to a swappable input. Inside its own foundries, peers like Tata Steel, JSW and Vedanta have moved through hundreds of AI deployments and billions in measured value while AIA's public posture remains alloy and metallurgy. The data is unambiguous: AI value capture is 12x higher when the founder is personally engaged. The strategic case for the Shah family and the AIA board investing in owner-level AI fluency is not a productivity story; it is a survival-of-pricing-power story.
