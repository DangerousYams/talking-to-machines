# Marketing for AIA Engineering: A Deep Dive

## TL;DR

AIA Engineering runs the marketing posture of a confident B2B foundry that has never had to fight for buyer attention. Their LinkedIn page (10.7K followers) is the weakest in the peer set on every measurable dimension: no named expert voices, no customer mine call-outs, no recurring technical case studies, no video, no carousels. Bhadresh Shah is essentially invisible online. Vega Industries (the export arm selling to 120+ countries) duplicates the same content in English only. Meanwhile Weir (~298K followers), FLS Mining, Metso (~468K) and ABB Process Automation (~440K) run a clear, repeatable playbook of "named expert + named mine + a real number." Even direct peers Molycop (~34K) and Magotteaux (~23K) have figured out the formula. AIA has the underlying data (4,000+ customers, 40 years of mine-by-mine wear records) and none of the visible output.

## The single most-copyable archetype

A landscape photograph of a real customer mill (interior or liner close-up) overlaid with one number ("+12% throughput, -8% kWh/t, Plant X, India"), captioned with a 3-paragraph technical write-up authored by a named AIA metallurgist, tagged to the customer where permitted, ending with a link to a longer case PDF. Weir, Metso, FLS Mining and Molycop all converge on this format. AIA does none of it.

## The five biggest gaps AI could close

1. **Customer case-study factory.** AIA executes hundreds of mill conversions a year and publishes nearly zero quantified outcomes (kWh/t saved, wear-rate ppm reduction, $/t improvement). AI can ghost-write 50+ anonymized case studies from internal trial reports, structured for LinkedIn carousels and gated PDFs.
2. **Founder voice.** Magotteaux's leadership is more visible. An AI-assisted ghostwriting cadence (1 long post + 2 reshares/week) on hi-chrome metallurgy, India manufacturing, and the energy/CO2 angle of grinding would compound fast against a near-zero baseline.
3. **Vega regional language content.** Vega sells in 120+ countries but posts only in English. AI-translated Spanish (LATAM copper), Portuguese (Brazil iron ore), Russian/Kazakh (CIS) and Arabic versions of the same content would 10x reach in actual buyer geographies for trivial cost.
4. **Foundry "how it's made" video.** Zero plant-tour, pour, heat-treat, or DEM-modelling video discoverable. AI-driven script + edit + voiceover pipelines can ship 2 short videos/week from existing GoPro/phone footage.
5. **Sustainability narrative.** Lower wear rate = less media replacement = less CO2 per ton milled. AIA has the data but publishes no Scope-3-style story. AI can synthesize internal trial data into a quarterly "Grinding Decarbonization Index" that mining ESG buyers would actually share.

## The 90-day minimum viable AI marketing motion

**Week 1:** ICP workshop. Tier 1 (top 50 named mining + cement accounts), Tier 2 (next 200), Tier 3 (long tail). Stand up Sales Navigator + Apollo (or Clay) + Enterprise Claude with no-training clause. Audit existing technical content; pick 10 anchor assets to rebuild.

**Weeks 2-4:** GTM engineer builds Clay tables for Tier 1 with enriched buying committees (mill superintendent, comminution engineer, COO, procurement). Launch LinkedIn page refresh + 30-day post calendar in CEO/CTO voice. Stand up employee advocacy (DSMN8 or GaggleAMP); onboard 15 field engineers across regions. Build a Claude account-briefing agent loaded with public reports and the sales playbook; sales reps get a one-page brief before every Tier 1 call.

**Weeks 5-8:** First 1:few LinkedIn campaign: 5 account clusters ("Chilean copper majors," "Indian cement groups," "Australian iron ore"), document ad + InMail, custom landing page per cluster. Weekly Perplexity/Claude competitive brief. Publish 2 long-form technical pieces co-authored by internal SME + AI; one anchored to a real wear-life study.

**Weeks 9-12:** First measured ABM scoreboard: meetings booked with Tier 1 named accounts, named-account pipeline, LinkedIn engagement from named buying-committee members, employee-advocacy reach. One executive video + one customer case-study video using Runway/Sora for B-roll, real plant footage for hero shots.

## The hard truths

- Generic AI whitepapers don't move pipeline. 50% of buyers stop reading the moment a post feels machine-generated; 46% trust a brand less when they discover undisclosed AI use.
- Mass LinkedIn DMs are now negative-ROI. Use AI to research and draft; let a human send a small number of high-quality messages.
- "Sounds like ChatGPT" is fatal in technical sales. Mill superintendents have been reading vendor literature for 25 years. The tells (em-dashes, "delve," "moreover," tidy 3-bullet structures) destroy credibility.
- Public LLMs can leak proprietary alloy chemistry. Use enterprise tiers with no-training contracts; build internal RAG over a sanitized technical corpus; legal review before any technical post mentioning customer process data.
- Engineers smell slop. AI helps you produce more practitioner content faster; it cannot replace the practitioner.
- Gartner projects 90% of B2B buying mediated by AI agents by 2028, $15T of spend. Practical implication: spec sheets, wear-life data and case studies must be machine-readable, structured and indexed, not just pretty PDFs.
