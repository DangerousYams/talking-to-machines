# Adani Cement CiNOC: Group-Scale AI With a Single Brand

## The one-line approach
**Top-down, capex-heavy, group-shared infrastructure, branded as one command.** Adani built a Group-level AI Labs, anchored it on a single hyperscaler (Microsoft Azure), wrapped it in a single brand (CiNOC), and is rolling it across all 100+ MTPA of cement assets simultaneously while doubling capacity. It is the Adani Ports digital control tower playbook applied to cement.

## How to talk about this in 30 seconds
When Adani bought Holcim's India business (Ambuja + ACC) in 2022 for $10.5 billion, they inherited a digital programme called "Plants of Tomorrow" with a working command-and-control dashboard already in place. Rather than rip it out, Adani extended it. They layered their own Group-level AI Labs (sitting on Microsoft Azure) on top, retired the Holcim brand, and in November 2025 announced **CiNOC, the Cement Intelligent Network Operations Centre**, as the AI nerve centre for the combined 107 MTPA business. The first hard outcome is already on the books: a 2% YoY drop in cost of sales and EBITDA of ₹850/tonne in Q3 FY26. The pitch is "agentic AI teammates" running planning, predictive maintenance, dispatch and sales. CiNOC is broader than UltraTech's narrower kiln/mill control-loop AI from ABB, but UltraTech still has the deeper combustion-control track record.

## Origin
- **Pre-Adani (2019 to 2022):** Holcim-owned Ambuja + ACC joined Holcim's global **"Plants of Tomorrow"** programme in 2020. DCS, SCADA, drones, video analytics under "Digital Eye", AI, predictive maintenance, digital twins. Phase 1 produced the **CNOC (Cement Network Operating Centre) dashboard**, an Industry 4.0 visual command-and-control layer.
- **Adani takeover:** Bought Holcim's India business for ~$10.5B in May 2022.
- **Bridge phase (2024):** Ambuja/ACC publicly described "teaming up with Adani Group's AI Labs… AI models, generative AI, video analytics, optimiser functionalities." First signal that Adani was layering its own AI stack on top of CNOC.
- **Launch:** **CiNOC formally announced 3 November 2025** in the Q2 FY26 release. **CEO Vinod Bahety** is the front man (Ajay Kapur had moved from CEO to MD on 1 April 2025 and retired 31 January 2026). CiNOC is Bahety's flagship, not Kapur's.
- **Build partner:** Built in-house by **Adani AI Labs** (a Group-level capability that also serves Ports, Power, Energy) on **Microsoft Azure data lake**. Not TCS, not Infosys, not Accenture. Microsoft published a customer story confirming Azure as the underlying compute layer.
- **Form factor:** CiNOC is a software platform layered on top, not a NASA-style room. CNOC was the dashboard. "Plants of the Future" is the on-the-ground plant programme. They sit as a stack, not a building.

## What worked (early; CiNOC is six months old)
1. **Cost of sales down 2% YoY in Q3 FY26 (3% for 9M)**, EBITDA at **₹850/tonne**. Bahety credited the cost leadership journey enabled by digital + CiNOC.
2. **Sales force fully digitised**: ground force on smart tabs, automated transporter allocation, digital dispatch.
3. **Fleet stack live**: Axestrack telematics + ePOD + DIGIPIN giving real-time visibility from kiln to last-mile delivery.
4. **Logistics savings target of ₹530/tonne by FY28** is now publicly underwritten by CiNOC, with marine logistics scaling from <2% to 10% of mix.
5. **Volume + capacity ramping in parallel**: Q3 FY26 volume 18.9 MnT (+17% YoY, highest ever). Capacity 107 MTPA today, target raised from 140 to **155 MTPA by FY28**. The digital layer is being built while the asset base is doubling.

## What didn't (or what's not yet there)
- **Holcim's "Plants of Tomorrow" branding is gone.** The programme name was retired. Adani kept the underlying SCADA / DCS / Digital Eye plumbing but re-skinned the top of the stack as "Plants of the Future" + CiNOC. Not a rip-out, a rebrand-and-extend.
- **No public evidence yet of kiln-level Advanced Process Control at the depth UltraTech has** with ABB Expert Optimiser (kiln, calciner, cooler, raw mill, cement mill). CiNOC's published wins concentrate in **logistics, sales, dispatch, predictive maintenance**, the enterprise/commercial layer, not the closed-loop combustion-control layer where ABB has 200+ kilns globally and UltraTech runs deep. This is the gap.
- **Thermal Substitution Rate (TSR)** is the giveaway metric. Adani is still **targeting 30% TSR**. Indian peers using AI-aided combustion control are already at 20 to 25%; global benchmarks are 30 to 40%. So combustion AI is a stated 2026 target, not yet a banked outcome.
- **Leadership change risk:** the executive who oversaw the Holcim-era digital DNA (Ajay Kapur) retired 31 January 2026. CiNOC is now owned by Vinod Bahety, a finance chief by background, not a plant operator.

## What's next (2026 forward)
- **Agentic AI rollout deepens.** Bahety: *"AI will run deep into our enterprise fabric… every plant, process and person into part of a self-learning, high-velocity operating network… Agentic AI teammates fuse human judgment with machine precision."*
- **30% TSR** with embedded agentic AI for fuel-mix optimisation.
- **One Cement Platform**: Ambuja + ACC + Orient Cement merge into a single legal entity. CiNOC becomes the unified operating layer for the merged group.
- **Group-level AI muscle:** Adani committed **$100B over 10 years to AI-ready data centres**, with anchor partnerships with **Google** (gigawatt-scale Visakhapatnam + Noida campuses, ~$15B Google investment) and **Microsoft** (Hyderabad, Pune). Cement gets to ride this infrastructure essentially free.

## How Adani's path differs from Tata and JSW
- **vs Tata:** Tata built infrastructure painfully over seven years, alone. Adani inherited working infrastructure from Holcim, then layered Group-scale AI Labs on top. Tata's program is older and deeper; Adani's is faster and more brand-led.
- **vs JSW:** JSW patiently runs project-by-project ROI gates. Adani points capex at the problem and brands the result. JSW's CMD says AI won't disrupt his industry. Adani's chairman just committed $100B to AI data centres. Same country, opposite postures.

## The single most useful insight
Three Indian industrial groups. Three completely different approaches. Tata = infrastructure-first platform. JSW = patient OT-first build. Adani = top-down brand-and-Azure rollout. **None of them is wrong.** Each one fits the temperament of its owner. Which means the most important question for an AIA owner isn't "what tool should we buy?" It is "what is OUR temperament, and which of these three patterns fits it?"
